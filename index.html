Agent Usage Monitoring

Monitor and gain insights into how your AI agents are performing, and also evaluate the agents for accuracy. You can also track the interactions with your agents, understand real-world usage patterns, identify common errors, and measure overall performance.

  • Monitoring: Monitoring tracks performance and provides insight on how your agents behave in production. Monitor agents to ensure that your quality bars for response time and token counts are maintained over time. You can also see any errors logged here.
  • Evaluation: Evaluate agents before you deploy them, to ensure that they're ready for production. Test your agents for response correctness, response time, and token usage to meet your quality standards. You can also check the quality of answers generated through the document tool to assess how effectively agents utilize the retrieved context from the retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) metrics.

Steps to Enable | Metric Definitions

Tools > AI Agent Studio > Monitoring and Evaluation > Monitoring

  1. Select the three horizonal lines (Navigator Option) in the upper left-hand corner of the screen.
  2. Scroll down the list and click Tools.
  3. Then click on AI Agent Studio.
  4. Select Monitoring and Evaluation in the bottom right-hand corner of the screen.
  5. Select Monitoring

Business Value Dashboard

You can now measure the business impact of your AI agent teams using the Value dashboard. This visual dashboard provides an overall view of your AI fleet, performance by Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications products, and individual agent team metrics. These metrics are designed to help you estimate and visualize the tangible ROI of your AI initiatives. Track deployment, usage, and estimated time and cost savings for your published agent teams in one visual view.

The dashboard generates reports for review with automatically populated performance data. You can filter the data displayed on the dashboard by date ranges, agent teams, or business functions.

By monitoring key indicators, such as Total Time Savings, Total Cost Savings, Total Usage, and Total Agents, organizations can move beyond qualitative feedback to accurately quantify how AI agents drive operational efficiency and user satisfaction.

Steps to Enable

Tools > AI Agent Studio > Monitoring and Evaluation > Value

ERP – Procurement Policy Advisor in Self Service Procurement

The Procurement Policy Advisor agent provides users with seamless access to procurement policies, allowing them to ask questions about products and services while ordering what they need to get their jobs done.

Agent benefits:

  • Streamline access to policy and process documents when creating a requisition.
  • Improve compliance by providing policy guidance when users submit requisitions.

Steps to Enable

How to use the Agent:

  1. Click on Procurement
  2. Click on Purchase Requisitions
  3. Choose the radio button that say Procurement policy Advisor
  4. Click the button Ask Oracle
  5. Select any of the suggested questions
  6. Now ask the agent these prompts:
    1. What is the procurement process for me?
    2. My computer is 2 years old. When can I a get a new one?
    3. When is competitive bidding required?
    4. What purchases require manager approval?

SCM – Purchase Order to Sales Order Converter

Use the Purchase Orders to Sales Orders Converter to automatically convert a purchase order from a PDF file into a sales order. This AI agent will take the PDF files you upload that contain your purchase orders and convert them into sales orders.

Agent benefits:

  • Let AI do the tedious work. You don't have to manually convert a sales order into a purchase order.
  • Increase efficiency. Reduce data entry errors and automatically fix errors.
  • Improve timeliness. Automatically fix errors and get the order into draft status instead failing.

Steps to Enable | Activation Guide

How to activate the Agent:

  1. Log in as John.Dunbar (Our administrator).
  2. Select the three horizonal lines (Navigator Option) in the upper left-hand corner of the screen.
  3. Click on My Enterprise and then Setup and Maintenance
  4. Click the Task icon on the right side of the screen and then click the Search option.

CX Sales – Lead Advisor Agent

The Lead Advisor Agent is an AI-powered smart agent designed to help Sales Users interact directly with a lead’s context without navigating across multiple tabs or reports. By using Generative AI, the assistant intelligently responds to natural questions about a lead by analyzing a wide range of related data, including campaign history, recent activities, orchestration details, notes, and even custom fields. It can be launched via a smart action on any lead record. Whether you're preparing for a call, planning your next outreach, or qualifying a lead, the assistant helps you get precise answers instantly.


Agent business benefits:

  • Faster Qualification Decisions: Understand lead behavior, engagement, and readiness in seconds, leading to faster conversion cycles.
  • Reduced Manual Research Time: Eliminate time-consuming toggling between tabs and reports to gather lead context.
  • Smarter Engagement: Personalize follow-ups and conversations using contextual insights derived from the entire lead journey.

Steps to Enable | Activation Guide

How to activate the Agent:

  1. Log in as John.Dunbar (Our administrator)
  2. Select the three horizonal lines (Navigator Option) in the upper left-hand corner of the screen.
  3. Click on Configuration and then Sandboxes
